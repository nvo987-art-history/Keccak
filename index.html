Cryptographic Information

Keccak-256 and Whirlpool are cryptographic hash functions. They are designed to produce deterministic fixed-length digest values from arbitrary input data.

Keccak-256 produces a 256-bit digest, represented as 64 hexadecimal characters.

Whirlpool produces a 512-bit digest, represented as 128 hexadecimal characters.

Keccak-256 is not identical to SHA3-256. Although both algorithms are members of the Keccak family and produce 256-bit outputs, they use different padding and domain-separation conventions.

A cryptographic hash is not encryption and is not designed to allow recovery of the original input from the resulting digest.

Hash comparison can be used for integrity verification, but a matching digest by itself does not establish authorship, authenticity, ownership, provenance, legality or absence of malicious content.

Users remain responsible for selecting an appropriate algorithm and independently validating cryptographic results before relying upon them for security, legal, commercial, compliance, forensic or production purposes.
